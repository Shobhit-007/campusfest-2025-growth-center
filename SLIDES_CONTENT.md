## SLIDE 3 — How Will You Know What's Working?

### `[HEADLINE]` Measure, Diagnose, Adapt — Before It's Too Late

### `[SUBHEAD]` Scale / Change / Stop Thresholds for Each Strategy

---

### `[TABLE — the core of this slide, 5 rows]`

| Strategy | Critical Variable | Early Signal | Scale | Change | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Close Negotiating** | Days since last activity per deal | Day 3: No reply → AMBER | 3+ deals close in Wk1 → extend to proposal-sent deals | Offer 10% early-bird discount | 0 closes after 5 days → accept pipeline, redirect |
| **2. Alumni Intros** | # quality intros received | Day 5: <5 intros → not gaining traction | >10 intros, >60% meeting rate → ask each connector for 2 more | 5-10 intros, <30% meeting rate → improve pitch deck | <3 intros after 7 days → reallocate to LinkedIn |
| **3. LinkedIn ABM** | Reply rate on first 10 messages | Day 4: reply rate on first batch | >50% reply → expand to 30 more brands | 30-50% reply → A/B test 2 message variants | <30% reply → stop, channel not working |
| **4. Win-Back Lost** | Response within 48 hours | 48h silence = signal | 1 of 3 positive → try 2 more lost deals | Revise package terms if "interested but..." | No response in 48h → permanent stop |
| **5. Inbound Content** | Form submissions per day | Day 3: <1/day → not reaching audience | >2/day → double ad budget | <2/day but >0 → retarget ads, improve CTA | <3 total after 7 days → stop, focus outbound |
